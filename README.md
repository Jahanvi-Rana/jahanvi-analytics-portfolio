### 📈 Gold Market Intelligence & Predictive Forecasting System
*A predictive financial analytics engine forecasting domestic and international gold price movements by integrating macroeconomic indicators with ensemble models.*
- 🛠️ **Tech Stack:** Python, Power BI, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, Scikit-Learn, Feature Engineering
- 📉 **Business Value:** Harmonizes multiple global and local financial indexes to evaluate historical price spreads, equipping stakeholders with reliable asset tracking.
- ⚙️ **Technical Execution:**
  - Harvested and transformed multi-source financial timelines, performing robust time-series feature engineering (lag features, rolling averages) while enforcing strict outlier validation.
  - Implemented, tuned, and cross-evaluated state-of-the-art gradient-boosted architectures (**XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost**) to track trend lines.
  - Benchmarked predictive system performance using **MAE, RMSE, and $R^2$ metrics** to find the most optimal configuration.
  - Accompanied the engine with an analytical Power BI control room tracking price vectors, economic variables, correlation heatmaps, and future forecasts.
